Things I was wrong about

Every one of these cost me something.

  1. 1

    I thought a cleaner interface makes a product better.

    At Leadrat I spent weeks making the screens cleaner. A salesperson looked at it and said he still didn't know what to do first. The design was nicer, but the product was not better.

    Nicer is not better.

  2. 2

    I thought following the brief is the actual job.

    I used to treat the brief as the task. Now I treat it as a guess. If I don't check the guess first, I just solve the wrong problem neatly.

    The brief is a guess.

  3. 3

    I thought AI is a way to produce faster.

    Now I use it to poke holes in my own ideas early, so I don't fall in love with the first one.

    It's for arguing, not for answers.
