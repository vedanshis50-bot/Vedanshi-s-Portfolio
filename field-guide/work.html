01System

The algorithm that decides who gets the next lead

Found in
Leadrat, a CRM that real-estate brokerages run their sales on. Global lead assignment.
Kind
Real product work. Spec, interaction design and algorithm UX.
My part
Sole product designer, working with a PM, a backend engineer and QA.
Scale
Built for roughly 30 to 100 agents and 100 to 500 inbound leads a day per org.
What it looked like
A settings panel with some percentage fields.
Underneath
A fairness guarantee that has to hold with no idea how many leads will arrive today, and survive a manager asking “why did she get that one?”
Evidence
Worked simulation: 50/30/20 lands at exactly 5/3/2 by lead 10 Fewer reassignment tickets, faster setup: unmeasured No live data yet
Still unknown
Whether agents trust a rule they can't see. The tie-break is fair but invisible.

02Moment

The 200 metres between the train door and the right ride

Found in
KSR Bengaluru City station. Last Mile, a mobile concept.
Kind
Self-initiated. Field research, a working prototype.
My part
Solo: research, scope, design, prototype.
Duration
NEEDS REAL INPUT: drafts say both 2 weeks and 4 weeks
What it looked like
A navigation problem. Another routing app.
Underneath
A reassurance problem. People paid ₹300 for a cab when a ₹20 bus left 90 seconds away, because they didn't know which exit to take.
Evidence
Decision in under 60 s Baselines from field observation, not a validated sample Not yet tested at the station
Still unknown
Whether people follow a written instruction over a map when they're tired and carrying bags.

03Decision

The room where an idea gets argued with before anyone builds it

Found in
Boardroom AI, a working prototype.
Kind
Concept. Self-initiated, not a shipped product.
My part
Solo, end to end: concept, persona prompts, verdict structure, UI, build.
Built with
HTML, JS and the Claude API NEEDS REAL INPUT: confirm
What it looked like
A chat with AI personas.
Underneath
A structure for productive disagreement. Six opinions are noise. The useful part is where they contradict each other.
Evidence
Runs end to end on sample ideas No real team has used it on a real decision
Still unknown

04Commitment

The commitment behind a simple fixed-deposit form

Found in
A mobile banking fixed-deposit flow.
Kind
Concept redesign, tested as a prototype.
My part
Research, UX, UI, prototyping and usability testing.
Research
6 interviews, a competitor review, an unmoderated test with 6 users.
What it looked like
A form: amount, tenure, confirm.
Underneath
A confidence problem. The flows asked for a decision before they showed its consequences.
Evidence
2 of 6 read the senior-citizen rate correctly first time The fix for that is proposed, not re-tested No business impact measured
Still unknown
The ease-of-use result is recorded as both 6/6 and 3/6 in my own notes NEEDS REAL INPUT
