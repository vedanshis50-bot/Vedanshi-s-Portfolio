Corrections

Three things I believed, and what changed my mind.

  1. C.01

    I thoughtA cleaner interface makes a product better.

    Nicer is not better.

    At Leadrat I spent weeks making the screens cleaner. A salesperson looked at it and said he still did not know what to do first. The design was nicer. The product was not better.

  2. C.02

    I thoughtFollow the brief. It is the actual job.

    The brief is a guess.

    I used to treat a brief as a task. If I don't check the guess first, I just solve the wrong problem neatly.

  3. C.03

    I thoughtAI is a way to produce faster.

    It is for arguing, not for answers.

    Now I use it to poke holes in my own ideas early, so I don't fall for the first one. Investigation 03 is what happened when I took that seriously.
